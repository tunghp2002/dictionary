## Generated English-Vietnamese content

Vietnamese meanings, English descriptions, bilingual examples, and
collocations are project-generated from pinned Open English WordNet sense
context with OpenAI models and deterministic project validators. They are
included in the dataset license to the extent copyright or database rights
exist.
